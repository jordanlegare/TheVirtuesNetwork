# [[Character Development|Atomic Note: Character Development]]

[[Character]] develops through [[Practice]] and [[Experience]] toward [[Virtue]]. [[Character]] is not innate but [[Cultivated]] through [[Habit]] formation. Each [[Virtue]] in the [[Virtue Network]]—such as [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]], [[Charity]], [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Joy]]—has corresponding [[Character]] qualities that develop over [[Time]] through [[Discipline]] and [[Reflection]].

[[Character]] is the [[Foundation]] that enables [[Virtue]] to flourish. Without good [[Character]], [[Virtue]] cannot be sustained. [[Character]] grows through the [[Four Stages]] of virtue acquisition: [[Knowledge]] (understanding what virtue is), [[Assent]] (agreeing it matters), [[Practice]] (acting in line with virtue), and [[Habit]] (where virtuous action becomes automatic through [[Discipline]] [1][2][3].

[[Character]] is built through [[Small Habits]] that accumulate over [[Time]] into [[Virtue]]. Each [[Virtue]] in the [[Virtue Network]] has [[Character]] traits that can be developed through [[Practice]] and [[Experience]] to become [[Habit]]ual expressions of virtue.

[[Character]] is distinct from [[Virtue]] but leads to it. [[Character]] is the [[Cultivation]] phase; [[Virtue]] is the [[Perfection]] phase. [[Character]] involves [[Willpower]] and [[Self-Discipline]]; [[Virtue]] involves [[Grace]] and [[Habit]]. [[Character]] is the [[Path]] toward [[Virtue]]; [[Virtue]] is the [[Arrival]] that becomes the [[Start]] of the [[Path]] again [1][2][3].

## Daily Life Application

### Building Character
- **Small Steps**: Practice small [[Habits]] daily
- **Consistency**: Do the right thing consistently
- **Reflection**: Review daily actions [[Care]]fully

### Growing in Virtue
- **Practice**: Act on [[Virtue]] principles
- **Experience**: Learn from outcomes
- **Reflection**: Adjust based on results

## Practical Character Practices

1. **Daily Review**: End each day reviewing [[Character]] development
2. **Small Habits**: Practice one virtuous [[Habit]] daily
3. **Mentorship**: Seek [[Mentor]] guidance
4. **Reflection**: Reflect on character growth
5. **Accountability**: Share [[Goals]] with trusted others
