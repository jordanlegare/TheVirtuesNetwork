# [[Effects of Virtues on Spirit|Atomic Note: Effects on Spirit]]

[[Virtues]] heal, strengthen, and [[Reform]] the [[Spirit]], transforming [[Person]] toward [[Goodness]]. Each [[Virtue]] contributes to [[Soul Health]] and [[Spiritual Growth]]. [[Virtues]] purify [[Heart]], [[Mind]], [[Will]].

[[Virtues]] heal from [[Sin]], from [[Wrong]], [[Suffering]]. [[Virtues]] [[Free]] from [[Bondage]] and [[Despair]]. [[Virtues]] [[Build]] [[Resilience]] of [[Spirit]].

[[Justice]] [[Restores]] [[Broken]] [[Relationships]]. [[Fortitude]] — [[Gives]] [[Spirit]] [[Strength]] to endure. [[Fortitude]] [[Builds]] [[Resilience]]. [[Temperance]] — [[Frees]] [[Spirit]] from [[Slavery]] to [[Desire]]. [[Temperance]] [[Brings]] [[Peace]] to [[Heart]].
