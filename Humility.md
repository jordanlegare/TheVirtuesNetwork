# [[Humility]]

[[Humility]] is one of the seven **heavenly virtues** (or seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin). It counters **pride** and teaches us to recognize our true place before God and others. [[Humility]] is not thinking less of ourselves but thinking of ourselves as we truly are: creatures with great gifts but also great limitations and dependence on divine grace [1][2][3].

> _"Humility is the virtue which enables us to recognize our true worth as creatures before Creator, to accept our limitations, and to serve others without seeking recognition."_

## Core Definition

[[Humility]] is the virtue of **realistic self-awareness**. It teaches us that we are not God, we are not the center of the universe, and we depend on God's grace for every good thing. [[Humility]] is not low self-esteem but **accurate self-knowledge**: knowing both our gifts and our limitations. [[Humility]] does not diminish our worth but recognizes it comes from God, not ourselves.

[[Humility]] is the foundation of all other virtues: without humility, all other virtues can become prideful. [[Humility]] counteracts pride and teaches us to serve, not to dominate. [[Humility]] says: "I have much to learn from every person I meet" and "God has given each of us gifts for their good" [1][2][3].

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The seven heavenly virtues (seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin) are:

1. **[[Humility]]** - Against Pride
2. **[[Charity]]** - Against Avarice/Greed
3. **[[Chastity]]** - Against Lust
4. **[[Kindness]]** - Against Envy
5. **[[Patience]]** - Against Wrath
6. **[[Diligence]]** - Against Sloth
7. **[[Temperance]]** - Against Gluttony

See the seven deadly sins and their remedies [3][4].

## Daily Life Application

### When Feeling Prideful
- **Pause**: Notice when pride arises
- **Pray**: Ask God for [[Humility]]
- **Recognize**: Our gifts are from God
- **Serve**: Do something for others
- **Learn**: Receive correction from others

### In Relationships
- **Listen**: Really listen to others
- **Learn**: Receive what others teach us
- **Forgive**: Accept correction without defensiveness
- **Admit**: Own mistakes and limitations
- **Serve**: Serve without recognition

### In Growth
- **Accept**: We need growth
- **Pray**: Ask for increase in virtue
- **Study**: Learn from teachers
- **Submit**: To God's will over our own preferences
- **Grow**: Through daily practice

## Practical Humility Practices

1. **Prayer for Humility**: Regular prayer asking for [[Humility]]
2. **Gratitude**: Thnk for gifts and limitations
3. **Service**: Serve without recognition
4. **Study**: Learn from teachers and mentors
5. **Surrender**: Submit to God's will not our preferences
6. **Repentance**: Confess pride when it arises
7. **Community**: Grow [[Humility]] in community
8. **Accept Criticism**: Receive correction without defensiveness

## The Acts of Humility

1. **Recognize**: We are creatures before Creator
2. **Accept**: Gift as given by God
3. **Serve**: Without seeking reward
4. **Receive**: What others offer willingly
5. **Learn**: From every encounter
6. **Submit**: To God's will above our own
7. **Surrender**: Let God work through us
8. **Trust**: That God will not fail us

## Humility and the Fruits of the Spirit

The fruits of the Spirit (1 Corinthians 13:13) are the gifts of [[Charity]] and divine grace:

1. **[[Joy]]** - [[Humility]] is joyful in dependence
2. **[[Peace]]** - [[Humility]] brings inner peace
3. **[[Patience]]** - [[Humility]] endures without complaint
4. **[[Gentleness]]** - [[Humility]] deals gently with others
5. **[[Kindness]]** - [[Humility]] loves without expectation
6. **[[Goodness]]** - [[Humility]] does good without merit
7. **[[Fidelity]]** - [[Humility]] is faithful to God alone
8. **[[Self-Control]]** - [[Humility]] moderates appetites

See [[Charity]], [[Diligence]], [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Patience]], [[Temperance]] and the other fruits of the Spirit [2][3].

## Barriers to Humility

- **Pride**: Thinking too highly of ourselves
- **Arrogance**: Superiority toward others
- **Self-Importance**: Thinking our opinions matter
- **Fear**: Fear of admitting limitations
- **Jealousy**: Wanting what others have
- **Worldly**: Placing value in visible status
- **Pretense**: False display of modesty
- **Insecurity**: Using false self-worth

Remove these barriers through [[Grace]] and community [3][4].

## Humility in the Tradition

[[Humility]] is the foundation of all virtues. Without [[Humility]], all other virtues can become prideful. [[Humility]] is the first step toward true virtue and the reception of divine grace. Saint Augustine said: "God helps those who help themselves" and "God helps those who help themselves" through recognition of our need for His help [1][2][3].
