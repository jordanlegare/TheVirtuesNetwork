# [[Charity]]

[[Charity]] is the greatest of the three **Theological virtues** (with [[Faith]] and [[Hope]]). It comes from 1 Corinthians 13:13: "[[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]], and **love** is abiding." [[Charity]] is not the mere human kindness but **[[Charity]] itself** (agape, the Greek word for "unmerited love"), the **greatest gift** God can give, which perfects and binds all other virtues together [1][2][3].

> _"Charity is the theological virtue by which we love God above all things for His own sake, and our neighbor as ourselves for the love of God. It is the greatest of all virtues." — Catechism of the Catholic Church

## Core Definition

[[Charity]] is **selfless love** (agape) that seeks the good of others for their own sake and for God's sake. It is not feeling but **virtuous action**: loving God, loving neighbor, and willing the good of others even at cost. [[Charity]] is the **remedy and the goal**: it heals the heart and brings it to its ultimate end.

[[Charity]] is the greatest because it binds and perfects all other virtues. Without [[Charity]], all our other virtues are dead: faith without [[Charity]] is dead faith, hope alone is insufficient without love. [[Charity]] is the medicine matched to avarice (greed), the greatest of the three theological gifts [1][2][3].

## The Theological Virtues

The three theological virtues come from 1 Corinthians 13:13:

1. **[[Faith]]** - Trust in God
2. **[[Hope]]** - Expectation of good from God
3. **[[Charity]]** - Love for God and neighbor

[[Charity]] is the greatest because it perfects the other two and binds all virtues together [2][3].

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The seven heavenly virtues (the seven remedial virtues, one per seven deadly sin) are:

1. **[[Humility]]** - Against Pride
2. **[[Charity]]** - Against Greed/Avarice (and the greatest of all)
3. **[[Chastity]]** - Against Lust
4. **[[Kindness]]** - Against Envy
5. **[[Patience]]** - Against Wrath
6. **[[Diligence]]** - Against Sloth
7. **[[Temperance]]** - Against Gluttony

See all seven virtues with their respective vices [3][4].

## Daily Life Application

### In Relationships
- **Love**: Treat others as yourself
- **Forgive**: Grant mercy to others
- **Endure**: Bear with faults
- **Serve**: Serve without expecting return
- **Pray**: For those you cannot love

### In Community
- **Give**: Share resources and time
- **Support**: Help the weak and vulnerable
- **Encourage**: Uplift others in difficulty
- **Reconcile**: Restore broken relationships
- **Advocate**: Justice for the oppressed

### In Self
- **Love**: Love yourself as God loves you
- **Pray**: Receive grace for more love
- **Study**: Learn better ways to love
- **Practise**: Cultivate love through discipline
- **Remember**: That you are loved

## The Love of God

[[Charity]] is God's love poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit:

1. **Faith**: We believe in God's love
2. **Hope**: We trust God will bring us to love
3. **[[Charity]]**: God's love fills us
4. **[[Charity]]**: We love God and neighbor
5. **[[Charity]]**: We become vehicles of divine love
6. **[[Charity]]**: Others experience God through us
7. **[[Charity]]**: We grow in likeness to God

## Practical Charity Practices

1. **Daily Prayer**: Ask for increase in [[Charity]]
2. **Charity**: Serve the poor and needy
3. **Forgiveness**: Forgive those who hurt you
4. **Service**: Volunteer time and talents
5. **Patience**: Bear with difficult people
6. **Generosity**: Give material and spiritual gifts
7. **Humility**: Serve without recognition
8. **Prayer**: Communion with divine love

## Love and the Fruits of the Spirit

The fruits of the Spirit (1 Corinthians 13:13) are the works of[[Charity]]:

1. **[[Kindness]]** - Loving others
2. **[[Joy]]** - Love brings joy
3. **[[Peace]]** - Love brings inner peace
4. **[[Patience]]** - Love endures all things
5. **[[Gentleness]]** - Love is gentle
6. **[[Self-Control]]** - Love does not seek self-gratification
7. **[[Humility]]** - Love is humble and patient
8. **[[Faith]]** - Love trusts always
9. **[[Hope]]** - Love is confident

See the fruits of the Spirit [2][3].

## Barriers to Charity

- **Greed**: Wanting only for ourselves
- **Avarice**: Hoarding rather than sharing
- **Anger**: Refusing to forgive
- **Judgment**: Criticizing others
- **Envy**: Wanting what others have
- **Pride**: Thinking we do not need love
- **Cynicism**: Dismissing love as naive
- **Fear**: Fear of giving

Remove these barriers through prayer, generosity, and forgiveness [2][3].

## Charity in the Tradition

[[Charity]] is the greatest of all virtues because it unifies them and is the gift of God's love. [[Charity]] binds and perfects all other virtues. Without [[Charity]], all our other virtues are dead. [[Charity]] is the remedy and the goal: it heals the heart and brings it to its ultimate end [1][2][3].
