# [[Patience]]

[[Patience]] is one of the seven **heavenly virtues** (the seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin). [[Patience]] counters **wrath** (anger) and teaches us to endure difficulties with calm trust in God's providence. [[Patience]] is not passive acceptance but **active endurance** without becoming bitter or resentful [1][2][3].

> _"Patience is the virtue which enables us to endure present difficulties with a calm spirit and a trust in God's providence, avoiding rash anger and impatience."_

## Core Definition

[[Patience]] is the virtue of **endurance without complaint**. It enables us to bear hardships, delays, and injustices without becoming resentful or angry. [[Patience]] is not weakness but **strength of character**: it does not mean enduring abuse or injustice, but responding to difficult situations with calm trust in God's plan. [[Patience]] says: "I may not understand this situation, but I trust that God will work it all out for good."

[[Patience]] is the opposite of wrath, the deadly sin that destroys relationships and harms the soul. [[Patience]] allows us to wait on God's timing and trust His promise that all things work together for good [1][2][3].

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The seven heavenly virtues (seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin) are:

1. **[[Humility]]** - Against Pride
2. **[[Charity]]** - Against Greed/Avarice
3. **[[Chastity]]** - Against Lust
4. **[[Kindness]]** - Against Envy
5. **[[Patience]]** - Against Wrath
6. **[[Diligence]]** - Against Sloth
7. **[[Temperance]]** - Against Gluttony

See the seven deadly sins and their remedies [3][4].

## Daily Life Application

### In Difficult Situations
- **Endure**: Keep going despite hardship
- **Trust**: That God has a purpose
- **Don't Rage**: Control anger when provoked
- **Wait Patiently**: Trust God's timing
- **Forbear**: Give others benefit of doubt

### In Relationships
- **Forgive**: Grant mercy to those who offend
- **Wait**: For the right time to address issues
- **Endure**: With those who test us
- **Support**: Siblings in difficulty
- **Be Patient**: With others' growth

### In Waiting
- **Pray**: Ask for [[Patience]]
- **Trust**: That waiting is not wasted
- **Serve**: Use waiting time for others
- **Learn**: What suffering teaches
- **Rejoice**: In anticipated fulfillment

## Practical Patience Practices

1. **Prayer for Patience**: Regular prayer for endurance
2. **Scripture**: Read passages on patience and endurance
3. **Meditation**: Reflect on God's timing
4. **Service**: Use waiting time for good work
5. **Gratitude**: Thank God for trials
6. **Endure**: Sufferings for Christ's sake
7. **Forgive**: Those who offend us
8. **Trust**: In God's providential care

## The Acts of Patience

1. **Endure**: Bearing difficulty
2. **Forgive**: Forgive offenses without complaint
3. **Wait**: For God's timing
4. **Refrain**: From quick anger
5. **Trust**: In God's plan
6. **Support**: Those in difficulty
7. **Accept**: Necessary suffering
8. **Wait**: For the right time

## Barriers to Patience

- **Wrath**: Anger and rashness
- **Impatience**: Wanting immediate results
- **Complaint**: Grumbling under burden
- **Worldly**: Seeking comfort from created things
- **Fear**: Not trusting God's care
- **Haste**: Wanting things sooner
- **Frustration**: Giving up when things don't go smoothly
- **Anger**: Not controlling anger

Remove these barriers through [[Patience]] practices and [[Grace]] [3][4].

## Patience in the Tradition

[[Patience]] is the virtue that enables us to endure with calm, forbearance. The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) and the three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are the framework for our spiritual growth. The seven heavenly virtues complete the picture. [[Patience]] is the opposite of wrath, the deadly sin that destroys relationships and harms the soul [1][2][3].
