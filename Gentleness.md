# [[Gentleness]]

[[Gentleness]] is the fruit of the Spirit that enables us to deal with others in a tender, considerate, and non-violent way even in confrontation. [[Gentleness]] is not weakness but **strength under control**—the capacity to be firm when needed but gentle in our treatment of others. [[Gentleness]] is the opposite of harshness, roughness, and violence with our words and actions. [[Gentleness]] is the opposite of [[Chastity]] in the way we treat others and in our demeanor at all times [1][2][3].

> _"Gentleness is the virtue by which we deal with others tenderly and respectfully, especially when others are vulnerable or in need. Gentleness is strength under control, not weakness."_

## Core Definition

[[Gentleness]] is the virtue of **tender consideration** in our treatment of others. It is not about the things we do for them but how we do them—with tenderness and consideration for their needs and feelings. [[Gentleness]] is the capacity to handle others delicately, to treat them with care, and to speak and act in ways that encourage rather than crush.

[[Gentleness]] is the opposite of harsh treatment but is not to be confused with [[Kindness]] which is the heart that seeks good. [[Gentleness]] is the manner of our treatment, not necessarily the outcome. [[Gentleness]] says: "I will treat others with tenderness and care, even in correction or confrontation." [1][2][3].

## The Tender Treatment

[[Gentleness]] operates as a virtue of tender treatment:

1. **Tenderness**: Handle others delicately
2. **Consideration**: Consider others' feelings
3. **Care**: Show tender care
4. **Tenderness**: Treat others tenderly
5. **Respect**: Respect others' dignity
6. **Humility**: Be humble in treatment
7. **Strength**: Strength under control
8. **Firmness**: Be firm when needed
9. **Restraint**: Control anger and harshness
10. **Love**: Love shown tenderly

## Barriers to Gentleness

- **Harshness**: Harsh treatment of others
- **Anger**: Uncontrolled anger
- **Cruelty**: Harsh treatment
- **Violence**: Violent behavior
- **Roughness**: Rough speech and action
- **Impatience**: Impatience that leads to harsh treatment
- **Pride**: Thinking we can be harsh
- **Unforgiveness**: Holding grudges that harm us
- **Fuss**: Fuss and fussiness
- **Worldly**: Worldly harshness

Remove these barriers through [[Gentleness]]'s practice and [[Humility]] [3][4].

## Gentleness in the Tradition

[[Gentleness]] is the fruit of the Spirit that enables us to deal with others tenderly. Jesus was called "gentle and humble in heart" (Matthew 11:29). [[Pope Gregory X]] lists [[Gentleness]] in the Rule of Wisdom as a central virtue for our treatment of others. The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) and the three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are the framework for our spiritual growth. The seven heavenly virtues (with [[Humility]], [[Charity]], [[Chastity]], [[Kindness]], [[Patience]], [[Diligence]], [[Temperance]]) complete our moral life [1][2][3].
