# [[Faith]]

[[Faith]] is one of the three **Theological virtues** (with [[Hope]] and [[Charity]]). It comes from 1 Corinthians 13:13: "[[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]], and love is abiding." [[Faith]] is the theological virtue by which we **believe in God** and **believe all that he has said and revealed**, and that which the Church holds to be revealed. [[Faith]] is not merely intellectual assent but **trust beyond evidence**, believing what we cannot see [1][2][3].

> _"Faith is a supernatural virtue by which we believe with a firm assent all that God has revealed. It is a gift of God, a movement of our mind and heart under the influence of grace." — Catechism of the Catholic Church

## Core Definition

[[Faith]] is the virtue of **trust** and **assent** to divine truth. It is not blind but **rational** in recognizing that some truths are beyond complete human understanding. [[Faith]] is the capacity to believe that which surpasses the natural reason's ability to fully comprehend, trusting in God's goodness and truthfulness.

[[Faith]] is not the absence of doubt but the **decision to trust** despite uncertainties. It is trusting in the unseen, believing beyond眼见 evidence, and accepting divine revelation with humility and love.

[[Faith]] is the first of the theological virtues to be infused into us by God. It prepares us to receive [[Hope]] and [[Charity|grace]] as the ultimate end of our lives [1][2][3].

## The Theological Virtues

The three theological virtues come from 1 Corinthians 13:13:

1. **[[Faith]]** - Trust in God
2. **[[Hope]]** - Expectation of good
3. **[[Charity]]** - Love of God and neighbor

[[Charity]] is the "greatest" because it perfects and unifies the other two [2][3].

## Daily Life Application

### In Prayer
- **Trust**: Even when things feel wrong
- **Believe**: That God is working for good
- **Submit**: Willingness to God's plan
- **Prayer**: Ask for increase in [[Faith]]
- **Worship**: Adoring God's greatness

### In Trials
- **Trust**: Even in suffering, God is working
- **Believe**: That God can do all things
- **Submit**: Accept God's will despite difficulty
- **Hope**: That suffering is not in vain
- **Persevere**: Continue in faith despite temptation

### In Relationships
- **Trust**: Others as God's instruments
- **Forgive**: Through faith in God's mercy
- **Serve**: Through faith in God's love
- **Pray**: For others' growth in virtue
- **Love**: Through faith in divine love

## Practical Faith Practices

1. **Prayer**: Regular prayer deepens [[Faith]]
2. **Scripture**: Read and meditate on God's word
3. **Worship**: Participate in church worship
4. **Sacrifice**: Give to increase [[Faith]]
5. **Study**: Learn more about God's truth
6. **Community**: Grow [[Faith]] in community
7. **Service**: Serve others to deepen trust in God
8. **Gratitude**: Thank God for all gifts

## The Acts of Faith

1. **Creed**: Believe in the articles of faith
2. **Hope**: Trust that God will save
3. **Charity**: Trust that God's love perfects us
4. **Prayer**: Request increase in [[Faith]]
5. **Worship**: Adore God's holiness
6. **Sacrament**: Receive grace through sacraments
7. **Obedience**: Submit to divine authority
8. **Sacrifice**: Give up things for God's sake

## Faith Through Trials

[[Faith]] grows through hardships:

1. **Suffering**: Tests and strengthens [[Faith]]
2. **Persecution**: Proves the depth of trust
3. **Uncertainty**: Builds [[Faith]]'s strength
4. **Confusion**: Challenges but strengthens belief
5. **Skepticism**: Tests and fortifies [[Faith]]
6. **Awe**: Increases wonder at God's greatness
7. **Gratitude**: Enhances appreciation for grace
8. **Communion**: Deepens relationship with God

## Faith and the Fruits of the Spirit

The fruits of the Spirit (1 Corinthians 13:13) are:

- **[[Charity]]**: Faith enables supernatural love
- **[[Hope]]**: Faith and [[Hope]] work together
- **[[Joy]]**: Faith in God's goodness
- **[[Peace]]**: Peace from faith in God's plan
- **[[Patience]]**: Endurance from trust
- **[[Kindness]]**: Faithful love to others
- **[[Goodness]]**: Faithful obedience to God's will
- **[[Fidelity]]**: Faithfulness to God
- **[[Gentleness]]**: Faithful treatment of others
- **[[Self-Control]]**: Mastery through faith

See [[Charity]], [[Hope]] and the other fruits of the Spirit [2][3].

## Barriers to Faith

- **Doubt**: Questioning God's goodness
- **Fear**: Trusting other things over God
- **Worldly Attachment**: Placing trust in material things
- **Skepticism**: Rejecting without examining
- **Pride**: Relying on self rather than God
- **Despair**: Believing God does not care
- **Ignorance**: Not knowing God's works
- **Hypocrisy**: Claiming to believe while not living it

Remove these barriers through prayer, study, and community [2][3].

## Faith in the Tradition

The three theological virtues come from 1 Corinthians 13:13. [[Faith]] is the first of these virtues, enabling us to trust God's revelation. [[Hope]] looks forward to the fulfillment, and [[Charity|Love]] is the "greatest" because it binds and perfects all virtues [1][2][3].

The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) are acquired through practice and teaching. The three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are infused by God and cannot be acquired but must be received through grace [2][3].
