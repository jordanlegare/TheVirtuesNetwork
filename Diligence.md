# [[Diligence]]

[[Diligence]] is one of the seven **heavenly virtues** (the seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin). [[Diligence]] counters **sloth** (acedia, spiritual laziness) and teaches us to apply ourselves faithfully and earnestly to tasks and duties. [[Diligence]] is not mere busyness but **faithful application** to one's duties, doing what needs to be done with care and perseverance [1][2][3].

> _"Diligence is the virtue of faithfully applying ourselves to our duties and tasks, pursuing excellence with care and consistency, not slothfully or perfunctorily."_

## Core Definition

[[Diligence]] is the virtue of **faithful effort** and **thorough execution**. It is not about working more or faster but about **doing it well and faithfully**. [[Diligence]] is the persistent doing of what is right regardless of the difficulty or the likelihood of immediate recognition. [[Diligence]] says: "I will do this task well because it is my duty and it matters."

[[Diligence]] is the opposite of sloth (acedia), the deadly sin of apathy, spiritual laziness, and neglect. [[Diligence]] teaches us to apply ourselves with care and consistency to our duties, tasks, and responsibilities. [[Diligence]] is not driven by external pressure but by **inner commitment** to excellence in doing the right thing [1][2][3].

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The seven heavenly virtues (the seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin) are:

1. **[[Humility]]** - Against Pride
2. **[[Charity]]** - Against Greed/Avarice
3. **[[Chastity]]** - Against Lust
4. **[[Kindness]]** - Against Envy
5. **[[Patience]]** - Against Wrath
6. **[[Diligence]]** - Against Sloth
7. **[[Temperance]]** - Against Gluttony

See all seven virtues with their respective vices [3][4].

## Daily Life Application

### In Work
- **Be Thorough**: Do things well, not just quickly
- **Stay Focused**: Resist distractions
- **Persevere**: Keep going through difficulties
- **Be Reliable**: Do what you say you'll do
- **Seek Improvement**: Always try to do better

### In Personal Growth
- **Practise**: Use gifts and talents daily
- **Study**: Pursue learning consistently
- **Apply**: Put theory into practice
- **Persist**: Continue despite obstacles
- **Seek Excellence**: Do your best every time

### In Relationships
- **Listen**: Attend to others' needs
- **Serve**: Help without being asked
- **Support**: Encourage others' growth
- **Follow Through**: Do what you promise
- **Be Present**: Give full attention

## Practical Diligence Practices

1. **Start Small**: Begin tasks without hesitation
2. **Prioritize**: Focus on what matters most
3. **Plan Ahead**: Prepare before starting
4. **Time Management**: Use time wisely
5. **Eliminate Distractions**: Focus on one task
6. **Persevere**: Don't give up early
7. **Review**: Check quality of work
8. **Improve**: Learn from mistakes

## The Three Uses of Diligence

[[Diligence]] operates in three domains:

1. **Self-Care**: Tending to your own well-being
2. **Service to Others**: Helping those in need
3. **Service to Creation**: Working for the common good

[[Diligence]] directs energy toward all three uses faithfully [3][4].

## Barriers to Diligence

- **Sloth**: Laziness and neglect
- **Procrastination**: Delaying tasks
- **Distraction**: Losing focus
- **Apathy**: Lack of interest or care
- **Fear**: Paralysis from difficulty
- **Worldly**: Seeking leisure over duty
- **Despair**: Believing effort won't help
- **Self-Pity**: Excessive sympathy for one's needs

Remove these barriers through [[Grace]] and discipline [3][4].

## Diligence in the Tradition

[[Diligence]] is the virtue that opposes sloth (acedia, spiritual laziness). The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) and the three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are the framework for our spiritual growth. The seven heavenly virtues complete the picture [1][2][3].
