# [[Chastity]]

[[Chastity]] is one of the seven **heavenly virtues** (the seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin). [[Chastity]] counters **lust** and teaches us to integrate our sexuality within the context of truth, goodness, and love. [[Chastity]] is not mere sexual repression but the virtue that enables us to express our sexuality in ways that are free from disordered attachment and directed toward the good of others [1][2][3].

> _"Chastity integrates our sexuality within the context of truth, goodness, and love. It frees us to express love authentically, to welcome our bodies, and to serve others without selfishness."_

## Core Definition

[[Chastity]] is the virtue of **purity of body and mind** in all expressions of sexuality. It is the capacity to integrate our sexual faculties with reason and will, to desire others in ways that reflect their full humanity, and not merely as sources of pleasure. [[Chastity]] is not shame about our bodies but the capacity to use our sexuality **well**, directing our sexual feelings toward what is right and good.

[[Chastity]] applies to all stages of life and relationships. For single persons, [[Chastity]] teaches **purity** until marriage. For married couples, [[Chastity]] calls to **fidelity** and proper expression within marriage vows. For all, [[Chastity]] frees us from slavery to disordered sexual attachment and enables authentic love [1][2][3].

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The seven heavenly virtues (the seven remedial virtues, one counter-practice per seven deadly sin) are:

1. **[[Humility]]** - Against Pride
2. **[[Charity]]** - Against Greed/Avarice
3. **[[Chastity]]** - Against Lust
4. **[[Kindness]]** - Against Envy
5. **[[Patience]]** - Against Wrath
6. **[[Diligence]]** - Against Sloth
7. **[[Temperance]]** - Against Gluttony

See all seven virtues with their respective vices [3][4].

## Daily Life Application

### For Singles
- **Pray**: For strength in purity
- **Guard Eyes**: Respectful attention to others
- **Resist Temptation**: Reject disordered attachments
- **Be Faithful**: Remain open to God's plan
- **Serve Others**: Use gifts in faithful ways

### For Married Persons
- **Be Faithful**: Honor marriage commitment
- **Grow Together**: Build relationship together
- **Give to Others**: Serve beyond home
- **Communicate**: Healthy communication
- **Grow Together**: Build intimacy and trust

### For All
- **Pray**: Ask for purity
- **Study**: Learn about sexuality and virtue
- **Community**: Grow in faith and virtue
- **Support**: Encourage others' purity
- **Forgive**: Grant mercy where needed

1. **Prayer for Purity**: Regular prayer against sexual sin
2. **Holy Images**: Surround with virtuous role models
3. **Accountability**: Seek support from community

5. **Gratitude**: Thank God for grace in weakness
6. **Rest**: Adequate sleep to control impulses
7. **Study**: Learn about sexuality and integrity
8. **Support**: Build supportive community

## The Threefold Gift of Chastity

[[Chastity]] operates in three forms:

1. **Purity** - For those not in marriage
2. **Fidelity** - For those in marriage
3. **Chaste Desire** - For all to desire others as whole persons

[[Chastity]] directs our sexuality toward truth and goodness [3][4].

## Barriers to Chastity

- **Lust**: Disordered sexual desire
- **Impurity**: Thoughts and actions contrary to purity
- **Addiction**: Slavery to sexual sin
- **Shame**: Believing we are unworthy
- **Worldliness**: Accepting disordered values
- **Isolation**: Lacking community support
- **Seduction**: Being led into sin
- **Ignorance**: Not understanding virtue

Remove these barriers through grace, accountability, and community [3][4].

## Chastity in the Tradition

[[Chastity]] is the virtue that integrates our sexuality with reason and will, directing our sexual faculties toward truth, goodness, and love. The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) and the three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are the framework for our spiritual growth. The seven heavenly virtues complete the picture [1][2][3].
