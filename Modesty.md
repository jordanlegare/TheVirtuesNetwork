# [[Modesty]]

[[Modesty]] is the virtue of **humble self-presentation** and proper boundaries regarding our bodies and ourselves. It is not shame about our bodies or inferiority but the capacity to present ourselves with appropriate dignity and respect for others. [[Modesty]] is distinct from [[Chastity]] though [[Chastity]] requires [[Modesty]]: [[Modesty]] is the outer expression of [[Chastity]] and [[Humility|humility]]. [[Modesty]] is not vanity but the proper order of ourselves and our presentation in the world [1][2][3].

> _"Modesty is the virtue by which we present ourselves with dignity and respect, avoiding both vanity and shame. Modesty respects the proper boundaries between self and others, body and soul."_

## Core Definition

[[Modesty]] is the virtue of **appropriate self-presentation** and proper respect for others. It teaches us to present our bodies and ourselves without seeking excessive attention or praise, without showing ourselves inappropriately, and without thinking more highly of ourselves than we are. [[Modesty]] is not thinking less of ourselves but thinking of ourselves in proper order with God's glory.

[[Modesty]] is the opposite of vanity, the sin of pride in ourselves. [[Modesty]] does not reject our bodies but presents them with dignity. [[Modesty]] respects the bodies of others and does not use others as objects for our pleasure or our display. [[Modesty]] is rooted in [[Humility]] and [[Chastity]] [1][2][3].

## The Two Sins of Modesty

[[Modesty]] opposes two sins:

1. **Vanity**: Excessive pride in ourselves
2. **Shame**: Excessive shame about ourselves

[[Modesty]] finds [[Grace]] in the middle: neither vanity nor shame but [[Grace]]-filled presentation [3][4].

## Barriers to Modesty

- **Vanity**: Excessive pride in ourselves
- **Shame**: Excessive shame about our bodies
- **Nudity**: Showing ourselves inappropriately
- **Voyeurism**: Looking at others inappropriately
- **Objectification**: Using others as objects
- **Self-Focus**: Excessive attention to self
- **Materialism**: Materialistic presentation
- **Carnality**: Carnal presentation
- **Worldly**: Worldly standards about presentation

Remove these barriers through [[Modesty]]'s practice and [[Humility]] [3][4].

## Modesty in the Tradition

[[Modesty]] is the virtue of humble self-presentation and proper respect for others. [[Pope Gregory X]] lists [[Modesty]] in the Rule of Wisdom as a central virtue. The four Cardinal virtues (with [[Prudence]], [[Justice]], [[Fortitude]], [[Temperance]]) and the three theological virtues (with [[Faith]], [[Hope]], [[Charity]]) are the framework for our spiritual growth. The seven heavenly virtues (with [[Humility]], [[Charity]], [[Chastity]], [[Kindness]], [[Patience]], [[Diligence]], [[Temperance]]) complete our moral life [1][2][3].
