# [[Character Traits|Atomic Note: Character Traits]]

[[Characters]] are [[Virtues]] manifested in [[Behavior]]. Each [[Virtue]] in the [[Virtue Network]] has corresponding [[Traits]] that [[Reflect]] its essence. [[Character]] traits are [[Habits]] that become [[Automatic]] through [[Practice]] over [[Time]]. [[Traits]] become [[Virtue]] through [[Maturity]] and [[Grace]]. [[Character]] traits [[Develop]] through [[Discipline]], [[Experience]], [[Reflection]].

[[Character]] traits [[Bridge]] [[Theory]] and [[Practice]]. [[Traits]] are [[Daily Expressions]] of [[Virtue]]. [[Virtue Network]] [[Virtues]] each have [[Distinct]] [[Traits]] that can be [[Identified]], [[Observed]], [[Developed]].

[[Character]] traits include: **honesty**, **reliability**, [[Integrity]] in action, [[Temperance]] in self-control, [[Justice]] in fairness, [[Humility]] in self-view, [[Charity]] in love of others, [[Faith]] in trust in God, [[Hope]] in expectation of good, [[Joy]] in gladness, [[Peace]] in tranquility, [[Wisdom]] in judgment, [[Patience]] in endurance, [[Diligence]] in effort, [[Giving]] in generosity, [[Kindness]] in caring, [[Forgiveness]] in mercy, [[Gentleness]] in tenderness, [[Generosity]] in giving, [[Modesty]] in presentation, [[Prudence]] in wisdom.

[[Character]] traits [[Interconnect]] with each other. [[Honesty]] supports [[Integrity]]. [[Reliability]] supports [[Trust]]. [[Self-control]] supports [[Temperance]]. [[Love]] supports [[Charity]]. [[Patience]] supports [[Humility]]. [[Joy]] supports [[Peace]]. [[Kindness]] supports [[Gentleness]]. [[Wisdom]] guides [[Judgment]]. [[Forgiveness]] supports [[Reconciliation]].

[[Traits]] [[Develop]] through [[Small Steps]]. [[Small Habits]] accumulate into [[Character]]. [[Small Actions]] [[Build]] [[Big]] [[Change]]. [[Practice]] makes [[Virtue]] [[Automatic]]. [[Experience]] [[Teaches]] lessons that [[Strengthen]] [[Character]]. [[Reflection]] [[Refines]] [[Traits]] and [[Improves]] [[Quality]].

[[Character]] development requires [[Willpower]]. [[Will]] [[Drives]] [[Action]]. [[Discipline]] [[Maintains]] [[Consistency]]. [[Habit]] [[Forms]] through [[Repetition]]. [[Time]] [[Solidifies]] [[Character]]. [[Community]] [[Supports]] [[Growth]]. [[Mentor]] [[Guides]] [[Progress]]. [[Prayer]] [[Opens]] [[Heart]] to [[Grace]]. [[Grace]] [[Transforms]] [[Wills]] [[Into]] [[Desire]] to [[Do]] Right.
